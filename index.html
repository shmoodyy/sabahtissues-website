About Us

A trusted UAE partner for everyday hygiene products

Based in Al Jurf Industrial Area 2, Ajman, Sabah Tissues converts high-quality parent rolls into finished tissue and napkin products. We supply retailers, wholesalers, hotels, restaurants and offices with reliable, competitively priced products, delivered on time.

  • Wholesale and bulk supply for retail and distribution partners
  • Products for the hospitality, food service and corporate sectors
  • Flexible order volumes and dependable lead times
  • Responsive local team for orders and after-sales support
